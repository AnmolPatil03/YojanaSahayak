Test Scheme Delta

Department of Fictional Women Welfare, Karnataka

This is a fictional scheme created to test Yojana Sahayak. It does not exist.

Overview

Test Scheme Delta is an invented support scheme for women with disabilities who want to start a small home-based business in Karnataka.

Benefits

  • A fictional one-time grant of 20,000 test-rupees for equipment.
  • Free fictional skills training for three months.

Eligibility

  • The applicant must be female.
  • The applicant must have a disability of 40 percent or more.
  • The applicant must live in Karnataka.
  • The applicant must be at least 18 years old.
  • The annual family income must be below 3,00,000 test-rupees.

Exclusions

  • Applicants who received a fictional Delta grant in the last five years.

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Disability certificate
  • Income certificate
  • Residence certificate
  • A short business plan

How to Apply

Apply in person at the fictional district women welfare office with the documents listed above.
