Test Scheme Alpha

Ministry of Fictional Education Affairs

This is a fictional scheme created to test Yojana Sahayak. It does not exist.

Details

Test Scheme Alpha is an invented scholarship for students pursuing a graduate degree. It is used to check retrieval, citations and the eligibility engine.

Benefits

  • A fictional annual scholarship of 12,000 test-rupees credited to the student's bank account.
  • A one-time fictional book grant of 3,000 test-rupees in the first year.

Eligibility

  • The applicant must be a student enrolled in a graduate (undergraduate degree) course.
  • The applicant must be between 17 and 25 years of age.
  • The annual family income must not exceed 2,50,000 test-rupees.
  • Open to applicants from the SC, ST and OBC categories.

Exclusions

  • Students already receiving another fictional scholarship for the same course are not eligible.

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Income certificate issued in the current financial year
  • Caste certificate
  • Bonafide certificate from the college
  • Bank passbook copy

Application Process

  1. Register on the fictional Alpha scholarship portal.
  2. Fill in the application form and upload the documents.
  3. Submit the form and note the application number.
